Don't go your entire life living your days only to please others. Don't waste time complaining about trivial things that don't add up to even a minute's worth of real energy. Life has so many ups and downs, so many obstacles that will try to derail your happiness — don't lose sight of what really matters.

*The point of it all* is to be free. Build relationships. Love. I'll say it again. **Love.**

I remember when I lost sight of the real reasons I do what I do every day of my life: ***never***. I may have a bad day. I may even want to be alone for a while. But I never lose the desire to live and be free. I never forget there are many less fortunate people in this world who give **100%** of themselves just to live. So why can't I? Why can't you?

The point of it all — don't fret or sweat the little things. Build for bigger and better lasting memories every day.
