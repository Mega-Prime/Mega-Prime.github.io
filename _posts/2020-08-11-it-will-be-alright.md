Someone woke up today with yesterday's weight of *their* world on their shoulders. That weight can come from many things — unresolved conflicts with friends and family, the loss of a loved one, the current state of the world, or things not being dealt with within self. There is, and always will be, something.

Today I'm not going to tell you to *suck it up, get over it, be strong and move on.* I'm not going to tell you these are just challenges hindering you from achieving your goals. All that may be true. But that is not what today's message is about.

Today I want you to know: **it will be alright.** Yesterday was tough. Today seems tougher. Tomorrow may even bring more of the same. Lean on those who love you. Who you trust. If today is a day that seems heavy for you, **talk about it.** Share the load.

I'm here. I promise. ***It will be alright.***
