In life we lose many things. *Money, friends, family, jobs.* But what is inevitable is that we will lose time if we lose hope that we will at some point **succeed** at conquering the challenges set in front of us.

Let's not sit idly by sulking in our losses, but stand tall, dust ourselves off, and move forward — putting our efforts into getting back on track as **winners**.

We all experience wins and losses. It is up to us to remember what it takes to get back up and move forward when life hands us some not-so-savory situations to deal with.
