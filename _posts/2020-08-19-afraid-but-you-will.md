Afraid of *change*? For some, that word — even the thought of it — is scary. Small changes. Big changes. Often life throws a lot of reasons for change your way. Some good. Some bad.

Change can be scary. It doesn't just affect you, it may affect loved ones. It affects your normal day to day. What was yesterday is and will be different today and tomorrow. You are afraid. Unsure. That is understood.

There have been times in my life I have had to accept change, and I was afraid. ***Afraid… but I will succeed.*** I will never allow change to derail the goals I've set and the opportunity I've worked so hard for. I have learned to trust myself. My foundation. My strength and will to press forward even in tough times.

In order to accept change you must understand it. In order to understand it, like anything else, you must be patient and learn. *Change your mind — it can change your life.*

Change is good. I know you are afraid… **but you will succeed.**
