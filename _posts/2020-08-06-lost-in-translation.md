Yesterday I asked a group of people a question. The words were simple — not long, complex or hard to pronounce — but the question itself had a deeper focus. Very much out of the realm of simple.

**Question:** *"What is the worst part about being a man or a woman?"*

Some were unable to think deeper than the surface of materialistic things. Some couldn't answer at all because they *lost it in translation*, giving answers like *"I love being a man"* or *"men are assholes."* Of course, that last one had nothing to do with the question posed.

This draws a **simple** yet *interesting observation.* All too often things are *lost in translation* — either because of a lack of listening, or unwillingness to listen, or a lack of **comprehension and understanding.** Don't lose focus on important things by tracking down problems instead of solutions, or wasting time on the trivial. Don't allow emotion to cloud judgment and cost you opportunity.

**Answer the questions asked to you.** The only way to do that is to start listening. Be sure you understand. Process and respond — or your opportunities for success will be *lost in translation* as well.
