Today I want to share what touched my heart. Happy birthday to me. The below is a poem my sister wrote for me today.

**"A Brother's Love"**

<p class="verse">They say a girl's first love is her father.
But when that picture is blurry, who's next in line?
What's there during the storm to ensure she is fine…</p>

<p class="verse">Who opens her mind and shares their wildest dreams
And teaches life lessons — no, things aren't at all
what it seems…</p>

<p class="verse">Who watches her grow and helps lead the way
Holds her hand and wipes her tears on a weary day…</p>

<p class="verse">Who makes her stronger, when she feels she can only bite and scratch
Uplifts her in praise and motivates her soul
through any match…</p>

<p class="verse">Who's her protector and her diary before she can even write
Understands her jokes and fears, believes in her with
all their might…</p>

<p class="verse">Ensures her peace and shows her all the beauty she has within
Treats her like a princess and becomes her first best friend…</p>

<p class="verse">When time ever runs out, he still will be there.
With memories, obstacles and achievements
they forever will share.</p>

I love you, Big Brother. 🙂

*…And I you, Lil' Sis.*
