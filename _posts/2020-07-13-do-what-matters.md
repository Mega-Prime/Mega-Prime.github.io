topic: "Work"

---
Every day you have so much to do. At home you do your best to keep your house in order. In the office, you have loads of work. In life, you have so many people you feel you need to please.

*Stop.* Do what matters.

It takes time, but spend the time to organize the realistic goals in your day to day life. Take the time to breathe a little. Don't hold this senseless thought that the *constant grind* and tireless work is the way to success. Hard work, yes, is the correct path — but **proper rest and mental reprieve are just as important.**

**Do what matters.**
