---
Sometimes we work ourselves into a rut. We do so much to achieve goals and to build self, but we often run out of breath forgetting to *breathe*.

Be ok with taking a break. If you cannot **breathe**, you cannot *live*. What that means is: if you spend all your time thinking *"if I only work today, every day"* — when that tomorrow comes that you were working for, you'll have run out of time to enjoy it.

Be ok with living today. **Breathe.**
