What makes an effective leader? What moves people to follow and believe in the path being created by others before them? Some will say **faith**. **Trust**. **Hope**.

I say *communication.*

To be a great leader, yes, you have to inspire. You have to have a knack for asking why — and when that why is asked, you have to be able to provide the how. Providing that *how* is the hard part. Being able to communicate a message, goal, plan, or even your feelings clearly and concisely helps others not only trust, but understand.

But the most useful and effective tool a good communicator can employ is knowing when and how to **listen.** I believe listening is the foundation of communication. *Listening to understand.* To gain *insight and knowledge*. Listening to share — to share in the opening of pathways you have not yet traveled.

**Communication. Don't lack it.**
