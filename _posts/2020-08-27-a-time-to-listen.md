title: "A Time to Listen"
topic: "Empathy"
summary: "If you have nothing but an ear to give, you'll be amazed how much it helps."
comments: true
---
Someone somewhere is going through something. No matter who that someone is, no matter where they are, and no matter what that something is — there is a time for *action*, and a time for *listening*.

Don't let the world **consume** so much of you that you lose touch with those who need your shoulders to lean on. If you have nothing but an ear to give, you'll be amazed at how just listening can help in so many ways.

Now is the time to maybe just ***listen***.
