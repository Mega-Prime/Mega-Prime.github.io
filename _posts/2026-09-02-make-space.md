Have you ever felt like everyday there is something new to take on? Like the todo list, the woes, the needs and wants are all never ending?  I certainly at times do.

Sometimes I need a reminder that, though others depend on me, and I do my best to make space for that. I must remember to allow others to make space for me. To support in the ways that help me grow and continue moving forward. Remembering that **those who are watched are not always seen,** I must make room for those I care for, to care for me. I'm not alone. 

What are you taking on, and are you making space to be supported? Cared for?

-Mega
