Here's something to ponder. How often do you take the time out to just *think*?

Yes, we all have daily cognitive activity where we need to make decisions and think on trivial things. But how often do we take the time to be deep in thought? How often do we shape our minds, mold them to a sharper focus?

Today… ***think.*** Tomorrow's success may depend on it.
