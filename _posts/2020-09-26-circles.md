title: "Circles"
topic: "Mindset"
summary: "Goal setting and a well thought out plan will keep you from running in circles."
comments: true
---
If you find yourself going round and round, over and under in *circles*, unable to gain any traction on **success** — stop.

Take a moment, find the nearest exit to the situation you are in, and revisit your plan.

Goal setting and a well thought out plan will keep you from running yourself in circles.
