What you do in life every day, from the time you are capable of controlling your actions to this very moment, is called *insurance*. This insurance provides you the ability to hedge bets against your success. If you fall a little short sometimes, the foundation of **hard work and effort** you've put in over time should — and will — still afford you new opportunities.

Be mindful of the foundation you are building. The choices you make, the books you read, the people you allow in your circle. All of these contribute to your success or lack of it. All of these are part of your life's insurance plan.

Keep your eyes on the right things that make you happy. **Energize** yourself with the right people who have your *goals and success* in mind, and who know when to *respectfully* help set you back on the right path.

Don't cut the wrong checks and end up with an **unhappy settlement.**
