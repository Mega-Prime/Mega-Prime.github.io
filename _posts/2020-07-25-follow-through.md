title: "Follow Through"
topic: "Work"
summary: "Always show up to opportunity dressed from the inside out for success."
comments: true
---
When you set great expectations of yourself with others, and they believe they will get the best from you — **follow through.** Always show up to opportunity dressed from the inside out for success.

What does that mean? It means you need to be confident, ready to teach, and just as importantly ready to listen, learn and understand. The effort put in to get the opportunities afforded to you is just the beginning. Following through and seeing things to completion is what builds **bridges of trust** to even more opportunity.

In relationships, following through with good communication and patience. In the workplace, with meeting deadlines and doing well on what's assigned to you. In life, with adding tools to your toolbox and applying what you've learned. Knowing when to *step up and stand out*, and always doing your best to never break promises.

**Follow through.** What you put out can always use you putting more in.
