In our lives we have multiple forms of input. From *social media*, *network television*, and the infamous *internet*, what our minds ingest more often than not controls the quality of our output.

**Control.** Manage the inputs in your life. Spending quality time with a good book, or learning a new skill to add to your toolbox, helps shape a stronger mind and more productive days. Controlling what your mind consumes contributes to the level of effort you are putting toward success.

**Alter.** We often find ourselves over-thinking situations, or consuming streams of negative thought and energy. Change the inputs in your life that are continual energy drains and are founded in despondency. Sometimes *less is more*, and in this instance, less pessimism is good for your productivity.

**Delete.** Understand what you want from your day, and remove the obstacles preventing you from achieving those goals. Whether that be procrastination, people, or whatever else in between, disconnect the inputs that chain you to mediocrity, negativity, and regression.
