**_What_** often occurs in relationships are disagreements. Arguments. Some trivial, some more serious. But inevitably they happen, and you must have the proper tools to deal with them.

**_When_** discord happens, it could be over words said, actions that threaten the health of the relationship, or most commonly a misunderstanding or miscommunication that takes a small flame of _upset_ and fans it to full-blown anger and all-out war.

I'm sure we've all experienced something, felt we had the right tools, and attempted to employ them to solve a problem. Maybe they helped. Maybe they didn't. But I can surely say in my experience there have been times I was totally confused about what the disagreement was about, how we even got to that juncture. I attempted to solve it logically and defuse the situation, only to make it worse — possibly because of my own ignorance of the issue, or something I didn't see in myself or the other person.

_"**How** do we solve these problems, Mega?"_ you ask. The answer is _I don't know._ I can't solve every problem, and in a relationship I surely **cannot** solve them all on my own.

What I think is important to start with is good communication on all sides. Honesty and openness without judgment or finger pointing. But most importantly — and many people forget this — when you are in a relationship, whether _friendship, romantic, or professional_, and you have a disagreement, remember that **it is you and the other person against the problem, not you against each other.**

_Give it some thought._
