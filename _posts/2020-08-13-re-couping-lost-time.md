There are going to be days where we have done nothing toward achieving set goals. Situations that alter our paths and affect milestones. *Life happens.*

But how do we recoup that lost time? How do we regain control of our timetable? **Proper time management and level setting.** That's how.

Taking the time to review our schedules and rework daily tasks is a great way to make up for lost time. Setting expectations with yourself and others allows you to get back on track, and back to putting your efforts toward building and grasping hold of new opportunities.

Remember the **four P's**: *preparation prevents poor performance.*
