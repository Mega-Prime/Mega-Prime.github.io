---
title: "Unconditional"
topic: "Mindset"
summary: "Success comes with dependencies. Everything has circumstance."
comments: true
---
I believe nothing in life is **unconditional**. Often we speak of *unconditional love*, or doing things for self and others without reserve. But everything has circumstance.

Even the idea of the word **unconditional** itself can and should only be reserved for law or contracts. When you think about it, even that is a state of affairs that in itself is — yes, you guessed it — a **condition**.

I say all this because success comes with *dependencies*. Dependencies based on the condition that you will put in the efforts needed of you to **learn, grow, and acquire** the right tools to build *prosperity*.

Don't get hung up looking for some unconditional value in things, or in others, that even with the deepest reflection within you won't find in self. Instead focus your *level of effort* toward creating **opportunity** on the conditions the world has set in front of you.
