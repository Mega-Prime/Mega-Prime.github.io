The going. What is it? The going is the *path* to your goals. And guess what? Sometimes the going gets tough. But you've got to keep pushing, putting in the effort to get those opportunities for success.

The going gets tough, and sometimes we feel like we want to give in to the negative thoughts, or the fears and scapegoats. **Don't.** You've got to go through what you are going through. In order to get to that goal, you've got to go down that path.

There will be times where that path is nice and smooth, sunny and bright. But when it gets rough and rocky, tough on your motivation, and dark as hell — you keep going.

**The going gets tough… but you are tougher.**
