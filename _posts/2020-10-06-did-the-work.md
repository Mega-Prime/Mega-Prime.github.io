If you *did the work*, you deserve the **success**.

I had an interesting discussion with a friend about waiting to be recognized for the hard work they had done. Feeling like you are not being acknowledged — whether that be through positive words of **affirmation, a promotion, or a bonus** — can be draining on your *motivation*.

I will say this: you **do not** have to be *patient* about your success. If you have done the work, you deserve the recognition. Take a step back, evaluate whether you have done what is required, and if you have to step away from your current opportunity for greener pastures, **do so.**

Did the work? *Don't be patient about your success.*
