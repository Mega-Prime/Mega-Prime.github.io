More often than not we talk about what makes us _unhappy_. This only brings more negative energy to our day to day lives.

I am not saying it is not important to _acknowledge_ things that may not be the best of situations in our lives — but only to an extent. Lingering on these things won't help.

**Speak what makes you happy.** Today is a day to recognize the things that put a smile on your face and warm your heart. It is **healthy**. It fosters a life of **exuberance**.

_Speak what makes you happy._
