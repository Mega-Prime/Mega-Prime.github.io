***Consequence.*** What is it? When we take a moment and think about the choices we make in life — who it affects, what opportunities it leads to, what doors it closes — we begin to see the real power of choices.

The term *choose wisely* holds so much weight that in moments of haste we can forget a choice can be life altering, and in some cases life ending. Our actions affect our families, relationships, careers, and society's view of who we are and what we can contribute to our communities.

Be mindful of your choices. That includes the *friends you keep, foods you eat, words you speak, and time you spend in places.* Make choices that empower you to strive for excellence and that add value to your life and others. Choose to exude the right energy for progression and for building lasting, positive rapport.

So what is *consequence*? Well, that depends on the ***choices*** you make.
