*"I can't."* *"I'm not confident with…"* Two phrases that hold many people from achieving certain goals, and lose them the opportunity to take hold of the wheel on the path to their success.

***Own your success.*** There are many roadblocks and people along the way to your greatness that will put you in situations for potential failure. Don't be one of those roadblocks. Building self-awareness, confidence and trust will propel you past these obstacles and reward you in more ways than one.

Owning your success doesn't mean you know everything. Doesn't mean you refuse to ask for help. Doesn't mean you have to do everything alone. It is quite frankly the opposite. Owning your success means you know when to get a second opinion, pick up a book to learn something new, or enlist the wisdom of others to impart new knowledge that helps sculpt a better version of you.

Owning your success means holding yourself and others who are part of your journey *accountable*.

Do that, and surely you can be confident you will **succeed.**
