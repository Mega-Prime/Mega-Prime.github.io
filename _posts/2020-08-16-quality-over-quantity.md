It's not the number of books you've read, social media *friends* you have, or times you've done one thing versus another. **Quantity** only matters in respect to the number of opportunities created, people you've touched in a positive way, or solutions to problems. And even that is dependent on one thing: **quality.**

It is the quality of the books you read that builds the mind. I'd rather have one friend I know I can depend on when it counts the most than a long list of unknowns filling a space on social media to *feel better* about myself. I'd rather take the time to do a great job at something once, and potentially never have to do it again, than repeat the process over and over to patch the lack of effort taken the first time.

*The quality of a man is not measured in the number of times he tells you he is great, but in his effort to consistently improve himself.*
