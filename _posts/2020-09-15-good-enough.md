Sometimes we make the mistake of giving up on something because we don't know enough about it, or it gets a little tough.

We spend days wondering how to do something, or even why we should. We start. We stop. We have questions, but we never ask for answers. We start to believe we aren't *good enough*.

Well, that's not true. *You are **good enough***. Not knowing something only means you have the opportunity to learn more about it. It means you can, and should, work at getting better at it. Another example of **level of effort equals opportunity**.

Don't sell yourself short on success, or on great life experiences, thinking you aren't good enough.
