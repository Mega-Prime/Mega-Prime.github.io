title: "Take the Filter Off"
topic: "Mindset"

comments: true
---
Ever find yourself not making any progress? You've been attacking something for years, and you always seem to drop the ball and say you'll pick it up at a *later date*? That sounds like procrastination — and procrastination has extremities.

Procrastinating doesn't necessarily mean you aren't a motivated person, or that you're incapable. Sometimes it just means you're being lazy about it. Sometimes you hold off because of an internal fear of failure, self-doubt, or worry that someone won't like it. Something small and trivial, yet it's holding your progress hostage.

*Well, it's time to take the filter off.* Look yourself in the face and be **100%** honest. Others will tell you what you want to hear. Some will tip-toe around the truth and offer little white lies to pamper your feelings. You need to remove the filter, give yourself a real pep talk, and step up so you can stand out.

**Take the filter off** and start to feel the energy of your **emotions set you in motion.**
