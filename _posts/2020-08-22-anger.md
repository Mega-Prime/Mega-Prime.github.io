There will be lots of things in life that affect us emotionally. Some make us happy, sad, confused. Some even excite us. But what about *anger*?

Anger can set us back. In the workplace it can ruin opportunity, and show volatility in your ability to separate logic from emotion and keep a clear head while working with others. In relationships it can set you up to express feelings and speak words that set long-term relationships back years, or ruin rapport you've built over time.

Anger can *destroy trust and instill fear*, causing others to find you unapproachable — which in turn places more obstacles in the way of your goals, hindering opportunity and causing others to overlook your efforts.

**Anger.** *Will it help?*
