The recipe for success includes, but is not limited to, *time, effort, understanding, teaching and learning*. In order to take the reins of **triumph**, you have to have enthusiasm.

The **energy to succeed** takes one major ingredient: *a plan*. You can work, work, work, but if you do not have a plan for your **physical** and **mental** health and well-being, you will fail yourself by falling apart.

Don't let the clichés of the world fool you that running your tank empty seven days a week is the way to success.

Repeat after me: *smarter, not harder*. Make a real plan. Refine it as needed. Rejuvenate self, and you will have the **energy to succeed**.
