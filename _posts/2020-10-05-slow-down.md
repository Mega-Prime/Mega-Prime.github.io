Sometimes in life we are moving too fast. So fast that we cannot see the next turn ahead, or we fly right by an opportunity. Moving at the **speed of life** can surely cause a crash.

*Slow down.* It's ok to get some fresh air and take a break from the hustle and bustle of today's high speed madness.
