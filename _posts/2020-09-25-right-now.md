The perfect moment doesn't exist, so stop waiting for it.

The time for success is not defined by *perfection*. There's a good time to start anything that needs doing, and that time is **now**.

*Level of effort equals opportunity.* Why put off for tomorrow what you can do today?

**The time is now.**
