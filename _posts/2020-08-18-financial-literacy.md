Stop wondering where your money went. Why you are *broke* all the time, or how to finance a car, a home, or your education. Many of these woes stem from not understanding how to properly **manage** money, and from a lack of self-discipline when it comes to attaining, spending, and tracking your finances.

Inadequate financial literacy can lead to a lifetime of debt-related issues that create serious problems in relationships, in your ability to live a healthy social life, and even emotional issues leading to depression and anger.

Take control of your *ignorance* around money — what it really should mean to you, and what you really should be doing with it. It is vital to living in a capital-driven world.

Read. Study. **Don't be financially illiterate.** It can destroy your path to success.
