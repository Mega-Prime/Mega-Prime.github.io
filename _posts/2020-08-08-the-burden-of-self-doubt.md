You've worked hard. You've put forth a lot of effort to open new doors to new opportunity. Then the opportunity presents itself and — boom — you doubt if it's for you.

There may be a few underlying reasons. Maybe you feel you'll be burning a bridge. Maybe you believe you're not ready for the success train to take you where you want to be. Leery of stepping forward through that door because you've *failed* in the past. Whatever it is, ***the burden of self doubt is heavy.***

Self doubt is a nasty counter-tool many use to boost their confidence in the **excuses and scapegoats** they've made for not leaping forward. Leaping can be scary. **Don't ever doubt your ability.** You've put in the work. You've taken the time to get this far by being everything you needed to be — and one of those things is *strong.*

**Don't let self doubt be the obstacle that derails your run to success.**
