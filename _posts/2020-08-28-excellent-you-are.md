There are moments in life that **teach** you. Moments that **mold** who you are and who you want to be. And then there are moments that show the world your *greatness.*

Take pride in those moments. Your efforts have afforded you the **opportunity** to revel in your success. Be humble, but let your beacon of eminence shine as bright as the star you are.

Be **proud** of today. You earned every bit of the accolades, acclaim, commendation and adulation that make you of ***excellence.***
