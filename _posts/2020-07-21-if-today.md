---
title: "If Today…"
topic: "Mindset"
summary: "If today were your last day, what kind of mark did you leave?"
comments: true
---
Ask yourself: if today were your last day, what kind of mark did you leave? What impression did you make on others? Did you add any value, or in your time, did you take more than you worked to give?

If today were the day everyone chose to listen, what is it you would say? Would you express what you are feeling with clear thought and good intentions, or would you blab about your daily entertainment news?

If today you could get back all the time you wasted, lost, or thought you used for productivity — what would you do with it?

In order to be successful, you must know what to do when **success** says, *"If today I give you a chance, are you ready?"*
