**Problem is…** you have bad habits. We often get into the habit of wanting this, wanting that, and then jumping face first into a poorly planned — *or unplanned* — project, process or idea. So what happens? We give up. Which translates to failure.

Don't let someone else's excitement about something be your *hype man* to **failure.** Setting yourself up for disappointment and wasted time also kills your motivation and your progress toward properly set goals. It zaps energy.

Take the time to do your due diligence and research. Pad your goals with real viable knowledge of whatever it is you're doing. The more you know, the easier the path to success. The more effort you put into building understanding and proper time and resource management, the more ***opportunity to succeed and grow.***

Turn your habits into action that translates to positive return on invested time and energy. Procrastination and scapegoats will never reward you. Efforts will.

**Problem is,** someone might read this and still make an excuse.

*Will you?*
