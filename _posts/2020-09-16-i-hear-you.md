---
title: "I Hear You…"
topic: "Poems"
summary: "Call on me when you need me. I hear every word you don't speak."
comments: true
---
<p class="verse">**B**rother take my hand, I know you are weary.
These days are cold, and nights are dreary.
Long and filled with pain. Lean on me, let me shoulder some of the pain.</p>

<p class="verse">**L**et me hold you sister, there is security here.
I will never judge you for the color of your skin, or the texture of your hair.
I will be a knight, I don't need any shiny armor.
I am a warrior for you, mothers, sisters, and daughters.</p>

<p class="verse">**A**ll that you have been through, it's ok to cry.
Just know that I love you. I am by your side.</p>

<p class="verse">**C**all on me when you need me, I hear every word you don't speak.
But I will never silence you. I'll protect you through this heartbreak.</p>

<p class="verse">**K**ings and queens we are. I promise, they'll see.
Until then just know, I got you, and I know you got me.</p>
