Today, be driven to conquer the fear of failure. Be strong enough to recognize your weaknesses, and challenge yourself to make them strengths.

Today, be the captain of a ship you know may at times sail uncharted waters, but refuse to give up on being an explorer of knowledge.

Today, make the loss of an opportunity be the motivator for new successes. Force the self doubt to pay for the times it held you back from the greatness that would have come from investing **100% of you in you.** Give yourself a reason to thrive by igniting the flames of your passions with *attainable goals* that lead to *attainable reward.*

Today… today be *bold.* Be the beacon of excellence you were meant to be. Be the leader who has set an example not only for self but for others who are lost and need a blueprint to the path of success.

Today, let the level of effort you have put in for opportunity shine through, so others can see **you** and learn to **be bold too.**

Remember — today, if you are not *bold enough, strong enough*… take my hand. I have strength for you.
