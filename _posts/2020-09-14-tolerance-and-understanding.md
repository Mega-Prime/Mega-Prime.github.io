topic: "Empathy"

---
Remember that everyone learns the world around them differently. Sees the world around them differently. Understands the world around them differently.

Choosing to accept people for who they are, and being willing to learn from their experiences — that's **tolerance and understanding**.

We cannot dictate for others what they **do not** feel to be truths for them.

> "There are no facts, only interpretations." — Friedrich Nietzsche
