If there is anything I am sure of, it is that in life there *will* be challenges. There will be ups, downs, overs and unders. We will hedge bets against our skill set and experiences, and fall short at times. There is no doubt in the fact that life has its difficulties.

But… ***with certainty*** I can say, giving up is **not** the answer. Giving in to the weight of the world is **not** the path to success.

***Level of effort equals opportunity.***

Remember: you are **persevered.** You are **great.** You are **capable.** You are **loved.**
