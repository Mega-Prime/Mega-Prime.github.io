There will be moments where you just need to listen to the people, and the world, around you. No words need to be spoken. Every question doesn't warrant an answer. Every statement doesn't need to be taken as some poignant moment, or something you need to spend energy contemplating.

Silence doesn't mean you don't care. Sometimes, ***silence is silence.*** It is all you have to give in that moment.
