*Communication* is a deadly art form. It can kill common misconceptions, break through moments of disparity, ignite the flames of team work, or help you order a pizza. We all know I love pizza.

But communication can also be detrimental to all of those things. Lack of it can **burn** bridges built, and destroy rapport between two people, or even groups of people. Assuming someone understands your needs and wants is not communication. It is exactly that — an assumption.

Be mindful of what others may or may not know. **Conscious communication.** What that means is: make the effort to *explicitly* open the line for listening and conversation. What goes unsaid gets **misinterpreted**, **misunderstood**, and leaves others **misinformed**.

*Be conscious of your communication.*
