Life is made up of many things, all lumped into three major categories: ***past, present, and future***. Very often our human nature has us stuck in the past while presently we are trying to accomplish greater things.

One thing that **destroys** productivity, blocks real opportunity and growth, and ruins relationships is *frustration and grudges*. These are the cornerstone of deterioration. They have the most adverse effect on productivity and can *blind* you from achieving the success you seek.

Whatever it is that you hold onto that boils within you — **let it go.** Turn it into positive energy, learn from it, and use it as a motivating factor for growth.

**Let it go.** It's taking energy you need for more important things.
