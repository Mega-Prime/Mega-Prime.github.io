The bad news is: *there will always be bad news.* There will always be something that tries to tear you down and break your cycle of success. Things that beat at your **soul**, your **morale**, and definitely your **patience**.

But the good news — no. *No.* The **great news** is that you have built a solid, lasting foundation of *knowledge, strength, patience, and integrity.* So as that *bad news* starts to creep into your path and tries to derail your goals, your efforts toward greatness will not be shaken.

Take a deep breath. Step back. See the situation for what it is, and proceed as necessary. You can't win every battle, or every race. But you can create better opportunity for yourself by showing and proving that when bad news comes, you handle it with **patience** and **intelligent** actions.
