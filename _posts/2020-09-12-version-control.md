---
title: "Version Control"
topic: "Communication"
summary: "The person the world sees is what you present."
comments: true
---
Remember, the best version of you will be tested daily. It is not the responsibility of others around you to keep you in check. It is you who should be cognizant of the face you present to the world.

This does not at all imply we should fake who we are — but what we show is not always *pretty*.

I know there are times where we might not be in the best of moods, or the best version of you might not be readily available. But remember: ***version control***. Your negative energy does not have to be what the world receives.

Be mindful of what you say and who you say it to. It's not always what you say that is the issue, but *how* you say it.

We are not always in a good mood. That is ok — we are *human*. But remember, **the person the world sees is what you present.**
