Issues in life occur. We are affected, and we either attempt to solve the problem or we bury it with excuses, lack of attention, or place it on someone else's shoulders. But if you are one who tends to see the same recurring issues in your life — **your way isn't working.**

In order to truly solve a problem we must first *identify* the root cause. Often we have clouded our head space with so many *scapegoats, excuses and procrastinations* that we can't see clear to the founding cause. Identifying the root takes a lot of **self reflection**, lowering of pride and ego, and riddance of excuses. Finding a true resolution takes real guts, and the courage to accept that your flaws or bad habits may be the reason you haven't been able to deal with recurring issues.

Take a step back. Look deep within to identify. Swallow your pride and crack the foundation of these things holding you back. Looking for a quick fix will only have you bouncing from one moment of mediocrity to the next.

Think **long term**, and you will have *true resolutions*, lasting success, and **opportunity.**
