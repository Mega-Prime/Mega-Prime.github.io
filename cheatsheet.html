Cheat Sheet

Tap a section to open it. Everything you need to post a thought, a quote, or a poem from your phone.

The 90-second routineevery day
  1. Tap the Studio icon on your home screen
  2. Tap Mega Thoughts, then New
  3. Title — two or three words
  4. Topic — pick from the list
  5. Write the thought
  6. Summary — one line, written after the thought
  7. Pull quote — the line worth screenshotting (optional)
  8. Tap Publish
  9. Wait 60 seconds, open urspective.com, read it back
Formatting2 marks
Type thisYou get
**word**Bold, with an amber highlight behind it
*word*Italic
blank lineNew paragraph
That's genuinely all you need. Bold is loud — two or three per thought, maximum. Any more and none of it lands.

Never type HTML. Never type the thought number — it's calculated for you.

Posting a quotesomeone else's words

Start the line with a > and a space. Put the attribution on the same line after an em dash.

> "Courage is grace under pressure." — Ernest Hemingway
FieldWhat to put
TopicQuotes
TitleThe idea, not the quote. "Courage", not the whole line.
SummaryWhat it's about, in your words
Pull quoteLeave blank — the quote is already the feature
Add a line of your own underneath. A quote alone is a repost. A quote plus why it hit you today is a Mega Thought. That's the whole difference.
Only quote what you can attribute. If you can't name who said it, don't post it — half of what circulates as Marcus Aurelius isn't.
Posting a poemline breaks matter

Poems are the one case needing something special. Normal paragraphs collapse line breaks; this preserves them.

Wrap each stanza like this:

<p class="verse">First line here
Second line here
Third line here</p>

<p class="verse">New stanza starts
after a blank line</p>
Open tag at the start, close tag at the end, one blank line between stanzas. Copy the block above and type over it — easier than remembering it.
FieldWhat to put
TopicPoems
Pull quoteYour strongest single line

Bold and italics work inside a verse block exactly as normal.

Summary rule: under 90 characters, and write it last. You can't compress something you haven't written yet.
Before you tap Publish5 checks
  • Title is short enough to text someone
  • Summary written, under 90 characters
  • Topic picked from the existing list
  • Read it once out loud
  • Bold used three times or fewer

You can always fix it. Every thought is a text file. Editing after publishing is normal and costs nothing.
